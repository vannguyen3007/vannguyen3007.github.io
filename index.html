In progress, since Mar 2026

CBCT-guided adaptive radiotherapy for rectal cancer

Daily cone-beam CT shows how the pelvis changes between treatments: rectal gas comes and goes, the bladder fills. I'm developing methods that register and contour these scans reliably, and say when they shouldn't be trusted.

  • Gas-aware deformable registrationDiffeomorphic deformation plus an unbalanced optimal transport term, so gas and bladder changes can appear or disappear.
  • Risk-controlled segmentationConformal risk control gives contour bands with guaranteed coverage and flags cases for physician review.

    Research Collaboration: Single-Cell Perturbation Screens

    Statistical Methodology Research

    Manuscript: “Closed-form sample size calculation for estimating perturbation directions in single-cell screens” (Biostatistics, under review). Project page

    Show detailsHide details
    • Statistical Methodology: Developed a theoretical framework using the Multivariate Delta Method, Taylor Expansion, and Asymptotic Variance to quantify uncertainty in drug-induced perturbation direction estimates.
    • Sample Size Estimation: Derived closed-form sample size approximations based on Covariance Propagation, Signal-to-Noise Ratio, and Directional Estimation Precision.
    • Drug-Response Analysis: Investigated the effects of biological and technical noise on perturbation direction estimation and drug-response signals in single-cell screening experiments.
  2. Mar 2026 – Present

    Research Collaboration: CBCT-Guided Adaptive Radiotherapy

    CBCT for Rectal Cancer Adaptive Treatment · USA

    Collaborators: Dr. Duong Thanh Tai (Spencer Hospital, USA); Dr. Dat Tran (Louisiana State University, USA). Project page

    Show detailsHide details
    • Gas-Aware Deformable Registration: Formulating CBCT–CT registration as a diffeomorphic deformation coupled with a Wasserstein–Fisher–Rao (unbalanced optimal transport) source term to model rectal gas and bladder-filling changes, benchmarked against VoxelMorph and TransMorph on contour propagation and dose accumulation.
    • Risk-Controlled Segmentation: Wrapping Swin UNETR and Bayesian Deep Ensembles in Conformal Risk Control with Mondrian and Weighted Conformal Prediction, producing contour bands with finite-sample guarantees on missed CTV volume to flag cases for radiation oncologist review.
    • Counterfactual Dosimetry: Using Neural Dose Surrogates, Bilevel Optimization, and Differentiable DVH Loss to quantify the dosimetric impact of residual segmentation errors.
    • Data and Validation: Exploring SynthRAD2025, COBRA2026, and institutional rectal CBCT/CT datasets with expert contours and radiotherapy dose records for geometric and dosimetric evaluation, subject to data availability.
