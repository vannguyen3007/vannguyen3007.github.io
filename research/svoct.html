GAN-based coagulation monitoring with speckle-variance OCT

Image-to-image translation to synthesize speckle-variance OCT images for monitoring blood coagulation.

Period
2021 – 2023
Where
BIEL Lab, Chonnam National University
Results
Average SSIM 0.7152, UQI 0.8863

The problem

Speckle-variance OCT (SvOCT) can show coagulation dynamics, but it needs large volumes of data and heavy processing. Learning-based synthesis could produce SvOCT-like images faster.

Approach

  • Phantom, in-vitro and in-vivo acquisition of OCT data.
  • Pix2Pix, CycleGAN and AttentionGAN trained to translate OCT into SvOCT images.
  • Image quality measured with SSIM and UQI, plus correlation analysis of coagulation signals.
