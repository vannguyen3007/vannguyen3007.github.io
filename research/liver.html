Liver tumor detection with multimodal imaging

Combining CT, MRI and PET to detect and segment liver lesions.

Period
2021 – 2023
Where
BIEL Lab, Chonnam National University Hwasun Hospital

The problem

Each modality sees liver lesions differently: CT and MRI show anatomy, PET shows metabolism. Combining them can separate lesion types better than one modality alone, but the images differ in resolution and contrast.

Approach

  • CT, MRI, ¹⁸F-FDG PET and ¹¹C-acetate PET for lesion characterization.
  • Segmentation and object detection models for lesion localization.
  • Fusion of anatomical and metabolic information, with regularization to handle differences between modalities.
