Research project

Bone Lesion & Tumor Detection

Exploring methods for detection and segmentation of lesions in skeletal medical imaging.

Research motivation

This project examines image-based lesion localization and segmentation methods.

Approach & scope

The portfolio describes thresholding, region growing, U-Net and Mask R-CNN applied in the context of MRI and PET imaging, including bone metastasis analysis.

This page summarizes documented research experience or a developing project. Numerical performance claims, publication details and dataset access are not provided without verification.

Source: Research Experience – Van Nguyen (supplied portfolio). Images reproduced from the original portfolio.

Methodology

  1. Prepare skeletal imaging data from CT/MRI and PET modalities.
  2. Apply thresholding, region growing or learning-based segmentation to identify candidate bone regions and lesions.
  3. Investigate lesion localization and standardized uptake value (SUV) measurements.
  4. Compare computed findings with available reference assessments.

Results and limitations

The supplied portfolio illustrates segmentation and SUV-analysis workflows; it does not provide a complete independently verifiable benchmark for this page.
